# Product and UX notes

## Discovery

The homepage puts a compact search and filter surface directly above results, so job seekers can refine an opportunity without losing context. On larger screens it remains near the top while browsing. Filters are native selects for clarity and mobile reliability; the active-state count is paired with a one-tap clear action.

Cards prioritize the scan order recruiters and candidates use: role/company, location and working arrangement, concise context, then salary and freshness. Hover and focus states provide feedback without visual noise. The small curated dataset keeps the interface realistic while making every control testable.

## Application and shortlist

The application flow is a focused modal with validation and explicit success confirmation. It labels the mock-data limitation rather than implying a real submission. Saving a role is immediate, visibly toggled, and retained in local storage. The dedicated saved page has a useful first-run empty state.

## Resilience and responsiveness

The layout collapses from two-column cards and detail content to single-column mobile reading. It includes a route loading skeleton, a custom not-found page, no-results recovery, semantic labels for controls, and visible keyboard focus behavior. The restrained green palette, generous whitespace, and clear type scale support a calm professional experience.
